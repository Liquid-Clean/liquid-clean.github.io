How It Works

Step 1: Activate

Click the Liquid Clean icon in your menu bar to enter cleaning mode. Keyboard and trackpad are instantly locked.

Step 2: Clean Freely

Wipe your MacBook screen and keys without accidental keystrokes or cursor movement.

Step 3: Exit

Press Command+Command. Input restores immediately—back to work in seconds.

Step 4: Repeat as Needed

Always ready in the menu bar. Zero setup, maximum convenience.
